Where we work

Six practices.
One purpose.

Stargate helps leaders build organizations capable of performing today while adapting for what comes next.

Our practices are not independent service lines. They are connected expressions of one philosophy: transformation begins with leadership, works through the whole organization, and should leave lasting capability behind.

A different starting point

We begin with the leadership problem—not the service category.

Organizations rarely experience problems in neat functional boxes. A stalled AI initiative may reveal unclear strategy. Slow decisions may reflect operating-model design. A culture problem may be an accountability problem in disguise.

We work across boundaries because the organization itself is a system. The right engagement may draw on several practices, but it should always remain anchored to the leadership outcomes that matter.
